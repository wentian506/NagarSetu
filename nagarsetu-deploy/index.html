Three everyday problems, one place to report them

The prototype starts with three categories, to be confirmed with the organisers. Tilt the cards to look around.

Uncollected waste

Bins not emptied or litter piling up. Goes to the sanitation team.

Broken streetlights

Dark stretches and failed lamps. Goes to the electrical team.

Potholes and road damage

Cracks, pits and broken edges. Goes to the roads team.

What it fixes behind the scenes

Not knowing where to report

One guided form replaces guessing which office to call.

No receipt or follow-up

Every report gets a ticket number and a public timeline.

Manual routing

Department rules send each ticket to a team and a staff member automatically.

Missed deadlines

A live clock runs on every ticket. Late tickets alert a human supervisor.

Unclear accountability

Every status change records who did it and when, in an audit log.

How to use NagarSetu

Pick a role. The four steps are in the order you will do them.

How a ticket moves

The same path every time, with a clock that runs on India Standard Time.

1. ReportResident picks a category, adds details and a location.
2. ConfirmNothing is created until the resident confirms.
3. Department consoleThe ticket lands there at once, with a ticket number like ticket001.
4. Staff assignedThe matching team's least busy staff member gets it.
5. In progressStaff update status and add a public note.
6. ResolvedThe resident sees the result on the timeline.

What is inside

Everything below runs in the live dashboard.

Real-time IST clock

Deadlines count down in hours, minutes and seconds on India Standard Time. A demo fast-forward is optional.

Automatic assignment

Tickets go to the department console and to the least busy staff member for that problem.

Live location or landmark

Share your location, tap the map or type a landmark. The pin must be inside Chandigarh.

Google map tracking

Turn on Google Maps with your own key. Without it, a built-in map and Open in Google Maps links are used.

Escalation to people

Reminder, supervisor and department head alerts. Tickets are never closed or rejected automatically.

Cited answers

The FAQ shows a source card, or says it cannot verify an answer.

Role-based access

Residents, staff and department admins see different screens, and the engine blocks other calls.

Dark mode and palettes

Light or dark, with five palettes. Contrast is checked for the default.

Rules we keep

AI suggests, people decide

AI only proposes a category. Residents edit it. Staff control status.

Departments set the deadlines

AI never invents a team, a deadline or a rule.

Simulated data

All tickets are labelled demo tickets. Nothing is filed with the real city.

Not an emergency service

In an emergency, contact the official emergency services directly.

Questions

Does it file real Chandigarh complaints?

No. It creates labelled demo tickets in the browser. Real filing needs an authorised, tested integration.

Does it resolve complaints faster than government portals?

We do not claim that. Speed depends on adoption, staffing and whether authorities cooperate.

How do I track my ticket?

Enter the ticket number, such as ticket001, under Track a ticket. Signed-in residents also see a My tickets list.

Is the map from Google?

When the site owner adds a Google Maps key, yes. Otherwise a built-in map with Open in Google Maps links is shown.

Which languages are supported?

English, Hindi and Punjabi. Translations are first drafts: have fluent speakers review them before real use.
